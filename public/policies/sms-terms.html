SMS Messaging Terms and Conditions

Effective Date: 10/1/26

1. Program Description

AnuPortal.com provides SMS messaging services to enrolled team members in support of its AI-focused CRM software and business operations.

Messages may include replies to inquiries, responses to requests for assistance, onboarding reminders, work-related notifications, and other authorized operational updates.

2. Consent to Receive Messages

By voluntarily opting in, you authorize AnuPortal.com to send SMS messages to the mobile number you provide for the selected messaging program.

Consent is not a condition of purchasing products or services. Where applicable, employment decisions should not be conditioned on consent to optional SMS communications.

3. Message Frequency

The frequency of messages may vary depending on your requests, account activity, notification settings, and business needs.

Where available, you may adjust your preferences or contact the company to request fewer messages.

4. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply to messages sent to you by AnuPortal.com and to messages you send to the company, depending on your mobile carrier and service plan.

5. Opt-Out Instructions

You may opt out of the messaging program at any time by replying STOP to a message from the program, where reply messaging is supported.

After your opt-out request is processed, you will no longer receive messages covered by that opt-out, except for a permitted confirmation message or other communications allowed by applicable law.

Other reasonable methods of withdrawing consent may also be available.

6. Help and Support

For assistance, reply HELP where supported, or consult the company handbook for the designated SMS support contact information and additional opt-out instructions.

7. Privacy and Information Handling

AnuPortal.com does not sell your personal information or share SMS opt-in data with third parties for their independent marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers acting on behalf of AnuPortal.com to deliver, secure, and maintain the messaging service, subject to appropriate safeguards. Additional information is provided in the AnuPortal.com SMS Privacy Policy.

8. Carrier Disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery depends on your carrier, network availability, device settings, and other factors outside AnuPortal.com's control.

9. Supported Devices and Numbers

You are responsible for providing a mobile number you control or are authorized to use. Please notify AnuPortal.com if your number changes or is reassigned so that messaging preferences and consent records can be updated appropriately.

10. Changes to These Terms

AnuPortal.com may update these Terms and Conditions to reflect changes in its services, operations, or applicable requirements. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel when required.

11. Contact Information

The company handbook contains the designated support contact information for SMS-related questions, privacy inquiries, and assistance with opting out.

By opting in, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to review these Terms and Conditions.
